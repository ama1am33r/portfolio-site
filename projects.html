Projects

Nottingham Green Festival

Role: Social Media Coordinator

As a Social Media Coordinator for the UK's largest one-day urban green festival, I developed interactive media, including video content and social media posts, designed to engage audiences and promote sustainability. In the lead-up to the event, I curated and scheduled a targeted promotional campaign to boost online presence and attract visitors, leveraging countdown posts, influencer collaborations, and digital ads. During the festival last year, I delivered live event coverage, sharing exclusive behind-the-scenes content to maintain online engagement. Additionally, I worked closely with volunteers, providing clear guidance to ensure smooth operations, and filming brief interviews with the coordinators about their roles and experience at the festival. I also actively interacted with the festival’s online community, addressing queries and facilitating discussions on sustainability, fostering a more positive dialogue around environmental issues and Green Festival’s impact.

Derbyshire Fire and Rescue Service

Role: Videographer

My primary role for this project was to produce a recruitment video and short-form content specifically for social media platforms with the objective to increase audience engagement and reach. The goal of this customer-focused campaign was to increase awareness of the Derbyshire Fire and Rescue Service (DFRS) and draw in potential candidates for part-time firefighter roles. As a way to ensure agreement with the client's goals, I handled the conceptual idea development, and filming scheduling, playing a significant part in the creative process. I was also responsible for recording compelling content for the target demographic. Post launch, I was responsible for monitoring social media analytics in order to: evaluate the performance of the content, measure interaction metrics, and improve our approach to increase audience reach and campaign efficacy overall.
